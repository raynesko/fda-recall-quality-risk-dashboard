# T1.1.2 - Define root-cause taxonomy v1 and QA glossary

**Sub-epic:** SE1 Domain Research & Problem Framing  |  **Story S1.1:** As a quality analyst reviewer, I want recall root causes and the DMAIC framing grounded in FDA/GMP concepts, so that the analysis uses the language of QA and is defensible.

## Description
Draft 12-15 mutually exclusive root-cause categories for reason-for-recall text. Starting candidates (revise freely): microbial contamination; lack of sterility assurance; undeclared allergen; foreign material/particulate; potency/assay failure; dissolution/stability/OOS failure; chemical contamination/impurity (e.g., nitrosamines, mycotoxins); CGMP/process-control deviation; labeling error; packaging/container-closure defect; cross-contamination/mix-up; temperature/storage excursion or inadequate processing; unapproved/adulterated product (e.g., undeclared active ingredient); Other/Unclear. Map each to the FDA six-system drug inspection model (quality, facilities & equipment, materials, production, packaging & labeling, laboratory controls) or to a food hazard class (biological, chemical, physical, allergen). Flag mappings you are unsure about.
